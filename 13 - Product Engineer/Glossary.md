# Glossary

Notes on terms to remember.

---

## Liability
**Meaning:** A legal responsibility or financial obligation — a debt or duty that must be paid/fulfilled.

- In **accounting**: debts and financial obligations a company/person owes (e.g. loans, accounts payable).
- In **law**: being legally responsible for an action or damage.
- Opposite of **asset**.

**Example:** "The company's liabilities exceeded its assets."
