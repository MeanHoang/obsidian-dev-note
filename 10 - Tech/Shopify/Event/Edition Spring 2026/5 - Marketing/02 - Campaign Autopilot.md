# Campaign Autopilot

AI-powered marketing automation tool tích hợp trực tiếp trong Shopify admin, giúp merchant chạy marketing cross-channel mà không cần quản lý thủ công từng platform.

---

## Cách hoat dong

Campaign Autopilot "uses AI to plan and run marketing on your behalf" - dua tren du lieu tu hang trieu store tren Shopify de dua ra recommendation theo category, region va growth stage cua merchant.

### Setup flow (5 buoc)

1. **Channel Connection** - Ket noi Meta ad account (moi hoac co san), Shop Campaigns, va email automations
2. **Budget Setting** - Dat monthly budget, he thong se phan bo across connected channels
3. **Rule Configuration** - Thiet lap guardrails: tu "approve every campaign before it runs" den "hands-off operation" tuy muc do tin tuong
4. **Automated Execution** - He thong tu tao campaign, phan bo budget ve channel hieu qua, dieu chinh theo real results
5. **Ongoing Control** - Merchant co the reject recommendations, modify budgets, hoac pause campaigns bat ky luc nao

### Cross-channel optimization

Autopilot "coordinates across all of them from one place. It moves your budget toward what's performing and pulls back from what isn't." Thay vi merchant phai tu toi uu tung channel, Autopilot xu ly tu dong.

### Diem quan trong

- **Khong chinh sua campaign hien co** - Autopilot tao campaign rieng, khong can thiep vao campaign merchant da tao thu cong
- **Khong tao AI creative** - Su dung product images co san cua store, khong generate hinh anh moi
- **Can thoi gian hoc** - He thong can thoi gian de learn store-specific patterns, khong guarantee ket qua ngay lap tuc

## Pricing

**Free** cho tat ca merchant tren paid Shopify plans. Merchant chi tra:
- Ad spend truc tiep cho Meta (Facebook/Instagram ads)
- Chi phi Shop Campaigns (pay-per-acquisition model, xem [[03 - Shop Campaigns]])
- Chi phi email qua Shopify Messaging (xem [[04 - WhatsApp & SMS Messaging]])

Khong co tool fee rieng cho Campaign Autopilot.

## Availability

- Early access cho eligible stores tren paid Shopify plans
- Channel availability thay doi theo location cua store

## Performance tracking

Merchant co the theo doi:
- Channel nao driving orders
- Product nao getting traction
- Cai gi underperforming
- Autopilot dang lam gi de xu ly
