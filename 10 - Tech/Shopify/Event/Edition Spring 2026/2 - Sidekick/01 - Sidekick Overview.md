# Sidekick - Shopify Editions Spring 2026

Sidekick la AI assistant tich hop sau vao Shopify admin, giup merchant thao tac nhanh hon bang ngon ngu tu nhien. Trong Editions Spring 2026, Shopify mo rong dang ke kha nang cua Sidekick: tu tich hop app ben thu 3, ho tro multi-task chay nen, tao customer bang mo ta, test automation Flow, cho den mo rong ra Apple Watch va moi man hinh tren mobile app.

Theo docs chinh thuc, Sidekick la "an AI-enabled commerce assistant in your Shopify admin, to get guidance, generate content, build apps, and complete tasks using everyday language."

---

## Core capabilities

- **Task handling:** phan tich data, quan ly orders, edit products
- **Content creation:** blog posts, product descriptions, images
- **Custom app generation:** tao app tu mo ta bang ngon ngu tu nhien
- **Third-party app integration:** tich hop app ben thu 3 thong qua Sidekick app extensions
- **Personalized responses:** nho lich su hoi thoai de tra loi theo context

## Cach tuong tac

| Phuong thuc | Desktop | Mobile | Apple Watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text chat | Co | Co | Khong |
| Voice mode | Co | Co | Co (duy nhat) |
| Screen sharing | Co | Co | Khong |

Tren desktop, giu **Shift + click Sidekick icon** de mo fullscreen mode. Tren mobile, tap maximize icon.

## Sidekick Pulse

Sidekick hoat dong theo 2 che do:
1. **Direct chat:** merchant hoi/yeu cau truc tiep
2. **Sidekick Pulse:** hien thi "proactive recommendation cards in your Home feed" goi y next-best actions dua tren store data. Dang rollout dan.

## Background processing & Multi-task

Sidekick ho tro multi-tasking thuc su:

- **Background processing:** "For longer tasks, Sidekick continues working in the background, even if you close the chat or navigate away, and notifies you when it's ready for review."
- **Multiple chats:** chay nhieu cuoc hoi thoai Sidekick cung luc
- **Track progress:** merchant co the "track its progress from Shopify Home" -- hien thi active tasks va status ma khong can mo chat window

## Follow-up questions

"If Sidekick needs more details to complete your request, then it asks a follow-up question." -- dua ra cac lua chon dang multiple choice de merchant chon nhanh thay vi phai go cau tra loi dai.

## Sidekick everywhere (Mobile)

Merchant co the truy cap Sidekick **tren bat ky man hinh nao** trong Shopify mobile app. Sidekick hoat dong theo context cua man hinh hien tai.

- Hien thi tren Orders, Analytics, Products screens
- iOS users co the them Sidekick lam **lock screen widget**
- Ho tro text, voice, va screen sharing

## Sidekick tren Apple Watch

- Mo Shopify app hoac tap Sidekick complication tren watch face
- Chi ho tro **voice mode**
- "After you ask your question, your answer is spoken aloud, and a text summary is displayed on your watch."
- Neu co nhieu stores, response dua tren active store (xac nhan tu complication label)

**Gioi han Apple Watch:** "Sidekick on Apple Watch is designed for quick answers. It can't do the following: Navigate to pages in the Shopify admin, Make changes to your store, such as editing products or updating settings, Generate content or apps, Use screen sharing or type messages."

## Actionable guidance (Home cards)

Moi lan mo admin, Sidekick dua ra goi y hanh dong cu the tren Home screen:
1. Thu hut khach hang (attract customers)
2. Cai thien conversion (improve conversion)
3. Thuc day mua lai (drive repeat sales)

Home cards la "blocks of information that change regularly to adapt to your business," bao gom:
- Updates ve Shopify features moi
- Loi khuyen su dung Shopify hieu qua
- Data-driven insights ve store performance
- Guides cho recommended tasks

**Insights section:** tao daily cho stores trung binh 10+ orders/tuan (6 thang gan nhat), toi da 3 insights/ngay, het han sau 24 gio.

## Sidekick creates customers & fills forms

Merchant mo ta customer bang ngon ngu tu nhien, Sidekick tu dong dien form. Fields do Sidekick dien se **highlight mau tim** de de review.

Ngoai customer, Sidekick con dien duoc:
- **Products:** tao/edit products, discounts, collections
- **B2B:** tao company moi
- **Orders:** edit order -- them product, dieu chinh so luong, quan ly line item discounts, dieu chinh custom shipping fees, set edit reasons
- **General forms:** dien field tren bat ky trang admin nao, dung reference images hoac direct instructions

"The order updates only after you review the changes and click **Update order**."

## Input features

- Attach hoac upload files
- **Target mode:** click icon target de chon phan cu the can Sidekick chinh sua
- **Mention:** products, orders, customers, collections, hoac apps
- **Reusable skills:** save prompts thanh shortcuts de dung lai

## Limitations chung

Sidekick **khong the**:
- Xu ly customer support
- Chia se private merchant data
- Edit images trong themes
- Thay doi bat ky gi ma khong co merchant approval
